How Spreads Work

Understanding the exchange rate

When you exchange currency, the rate you receive is based on the interbank mid-market rate plus a small spread. This spread is how exchange services cover dealing costs.

  • Interbank mid-rate — the midpoint between buy and sell prices on global markets
  • Buy vs. sell — we buy foreign currency from you at a slightly lower rate and sell it at a slightly higher rate
  • Spread disclosed — the full buy and sell rates are published on our rates page so you can compare before visiting

Payments & Refunds

How refunds and charges are handled

Refunds

Unused foreign currency can be sold back at the prevailing buy rate with no separate charge. Online rate-lock cancellations within 48 hours receive a full refund of any advance paid. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.
