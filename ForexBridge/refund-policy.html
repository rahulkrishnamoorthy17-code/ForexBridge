Refund Policy

Cancellations, buy-back and dispute handling

Home / Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026. This policy explains refunds, cancellations and buy-back for ForexBridge (demonstration brand) services. It is template content — replace with your organisation's actual policy before going live.

1. Currency exchange orders

An online rate lock reserves the quoted rate for the stated validity period (typically 24 hours) without any charge. If you cancel before the currency is dispensed and before collection, the full amount paid is refunded to the original payment method. Once currency has been dispatched or collection completed, the exchange is final and the buy-back rate applies instead.

2. Buy-back of unused foreign currency

Unused foreign currency notes in acceptable condition can be sold back to us at the prevailing buy rate on the day of the return transaction. Refunds are not issued at the original sell rate — the buy-back value depends on current market rates and note condition, exactly as it would at any forex counter.

3. International money transfers

Once an international transfer has been initiated, we cannot guarantee cancellation because funds may already be in settlement. If a transfer has not yet entered settlement, we will attempt to cancel it and refund the amount paid in full. Where a transfer has settled, cancellation and any recovery depend on the receiving bank and applicable fees.

4. Travel cards

An unused, untransacted travel card can be cancelled and the loaded balance refunded to the cardholder's Indian bank account at the applicable buy-back rate, net of any issuance fee. Cards that have been used for purchases or withdrawals are not refundable; the remaining balance can be reloaded or converted under the card provider's terms.

5. Doorstep delivery

Doorstep delivery is complimentary on qualifying orders. If an order is cancelled before delivery, any delivery charge is refunded. If the order was delivered and then returned by the customer, the buy-back rate applies and the delivery fee is non-refundable.

6. Incorrect or failed transactions

If we charge you for a transaction we did not complete, or if you receive incorrect currency or documents, we will correct the error and refund any amount wrongly collected as soon as the error is confirmed. Please report discrepancies within 7 days of the transaction.

7. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method — bank transfer, UPI, or cash — normally within 3–5 business days.
  • Refund eligibility documentation (receipts, notes or card statement) may be requested.
  • Buy-back and currency evaluations are conducted in your presence at a branch.

8. Raising a dispute

To raise a refund or dispute, contact us through the contact page within 7 days of the transaction with your receipt or transaction reference. We will acknowledge within 2 business days and resolve within a reasonable period, subject to the receiving bank or corridor partner where applicable.

Refund amounts for currency held in notes or cards depend on prevailing buy-back rates, which fluctuate with the market. Rates are confirmed at the time of the return transaction.
