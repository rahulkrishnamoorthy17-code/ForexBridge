About ForexBridge

Exchange Smarter. Travel Further.

Our Story

A different kind of currency desk

ForexBridge was started with a single idea: foreign exchange should be simple, transparent and easy to understand — whether you are a traveller buying dollars or a business settling an international invoice.

Every branch counter publishes clear buy and sell rates. Every transaction is backed by proper documentation. And every customer walks out with a receipt that explains exactly what they paid and why. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.

ForexBridge is a demonstration brand created for this template. Licensing, regulatory details and operational scope are fully configurable by the template owner.

  • Transparent buy & sell rates at every branch
  • FEMA-aligned documentation and clear receipts
  • Branch-level support — not a call-centre script
  • Licensing and compliance configurable by the template owner

Our Journey

From a single counter to a national network

A brief look at how ForexBridge grew to serve travellers and businesses across India.

1

The first counter

Founded as a single city-based currency counter focused on transparent rates and proper documentation for every customer.

2

Building the network

Expanded to multiple branches across major Indian cities, including airport locations for convenient travel-money pickup.

3

Going digital

Launched online rate checks, travel-card loading and a full remittance suite to serve customers beyond the counter.

4

Corporate desk

Introduced dedicated business-forex services with bulk settlements, trade documentation support and managed rate plans.

How We Work

Built around clarity and trust
