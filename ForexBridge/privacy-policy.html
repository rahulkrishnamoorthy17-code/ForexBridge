Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026. This policy explains how ForexBridge (a demonstration brand) collects and handles personal data. It is template content — replace with your organisation's actual privacy policy before going live.

Information we collect

  • Identity details — name, date of birth, and photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport) required for forex transactions and compliance.
  • Contact details — email address, phone number and postal address for order updates and communication.
  • Financial information — bank account details needed to process transfers and payouts.
  • Transaction history — records of exchanges, remittances and travel card loads, retained for compliance and audit.
  • Technical data — browser type, device and pages visited, used to keep the website secure and improve performance.

How we use your information

  • To verify identity and complete currency exchange, remittance and travel card transactions.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including FEMA-aligned record keeping.
  • To send rate alerts, order updates and receipts you have requested.
  • To prevent fraud and protect the security of our systems and your account.

Sharing your information

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Banks, corridor partners and settlement networks required to complete a transfer.
  • Regulatory authorities where required by law.
  • Service providers (for example, couriers or email platforms) bound by confidentiality agreements.

Data retention and security

Information is retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or as required by law. We apply appropriate technical and organisational controls to safeguard personal data, including access controls, encryption in transit and periodic reviews. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements. To exercise these rights, contact us via the contact page. We respond to verified requests within applicable timeframes.

Cookies

This website may use minimal cookies or local storage to remember preferences such as theme and layout. We do not use advertising trackers in this demonstration template. A live deployment should review and document its own cookie practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version with its effective date is always published on this page.

ForexBridge is a demonstration brand. Contact details are illustrative — reach us through the contact page.
